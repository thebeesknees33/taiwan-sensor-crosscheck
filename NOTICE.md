# Data attribution

## Central Weather Administration, Taiwan (CWA)

This project uses, and its test fixtures include, open data from:

交通部中央氣象署 (Central Weather Administration, Ministry of Transportation and Communications, Taiwan), 2015–2026:
- 顯著有感地震報告 Significant felt earthquake reports, E-A0015-001 / E-A0015-002
- 小區域有感地震報告 Small-area felt earthquake reports, E-A0016-001
- 地震目錄 Earthquake catalog, E-A0073-001 / E-A0073-002

The Open Data is made available to the public under the Open Government Data License, User can make use of it when complying to the condition and obligation of its terms.

Open Government Data License, version 1.0: https://data.gov.tw/license

Under section 3.2 of that licence, the rights it grants are void if this attribution is left out. Keep this notice with any copy or derivative of the CWA data.

## US Geological Survey (USGS)

Earthquake data from the USGS ComCat catalog (earthquake.usgs.gov) is a US Government work in the public domain. Some test fixtures were recorded by the USGS libcomcat project (public domain in the US, CC0 1.0 worldwide).
